Study Abroad Without IELTS: MOI and English Waiver Options

Some universities consider alternative proof of English proficiency, such as a Medium of Instruction (MOI) letter, prior English-medium education or another accepted test. This does not mean all courses, scholarships or student visa routes waive English requirements.

What is an MOI letter?

A Medium of Instruction letter normally confirms the language used for a previous qualification. Acceptance depends on the university, course, applicant history and the validity of the document.

Admission versus visa requirements

A university English-language waiver does not automatically waive immigration or scholarship requirements. Verify each requirement separately with the official institution and relevant authorities.

How to shortlist options

Check your degree level, academic record, course language, target country and budget. Ask the institution whether an MOI letter or alternative qualification is accepted for your specific programme.
