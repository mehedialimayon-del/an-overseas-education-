China Government Scholarship (CSC): Application Guide

The Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC) offers different application routes and funding arrangements. Eligibility, participating universities, degree levels, language requirements and deadlines depend on the specific programme and application channel.

What to check before applying

Confirm the official scholarship notice, the application category, eligible degree level, participating university, programme language and required documents. Some routes involve an embassy or designated agency; others involve the host university.

Documents and preparation

Applicants may need academic transcripts, certificates, a study or research plan, recommendation letters, passport details, language evidence and medical documents. Requirements vary by programme.

Funding and deadlines

Do not assume every scholarship covers the same expenses. Check tuition, accommodation, stipend, insurance, travel and the relevant deadline on the official call.

How A&N Overseas can help

Explore university options, compare programme requirements and organise an application checklist. Scholarship awards and admission decisions are made by the relevant institutions.
