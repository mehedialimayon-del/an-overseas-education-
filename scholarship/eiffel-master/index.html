France Excellence Eiffel · Master

France · Master

Funding coverage

€1,200/month since January 2026 plus eligible transport, insurance and settling-in services. Eiffel itself does not universally pay university tuition.

Eligibility

Foreign nationals including Bangladesh, typically ≤29 for Master at the 2027 call, excellent results and one of Eiffel priority disciplines.

Programme scope

French universities select and nominate, not self-submission to Campus France.

Duration

Up to 12/24/36 months depending on entry year/engineering route.

Application deadline

2027 national institution-submission deadline 6 January 2027; university internal deadlines earlier.

How to apply

1. Choose an eligible French university/master. 2. Contact its international office well before its internal Eiffel nomination deadline. 3. Submit the department’s required documents. 4. Only the French institution can formally nominate you.

Fees and conditions

Eiffel nomination has no universal student application charge; university programme fee may apply and Eiffel does NOT inherently cover tuition.
