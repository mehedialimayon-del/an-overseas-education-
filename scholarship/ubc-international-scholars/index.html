UBC International Scholars Program

Destination: Canada · Study level: Bachelor

Funding coverage

Need-based support assessed against educational and living costs and the student and family’s financial resources. The individual award is determined by UBC.

Eligibility

International undergraduate applicants who meet UBC’s entry requirements and can demonstrate strong academic results, leadership, community contribution and significant financial need. Check first-degree and school-leaving requirements for the 2027 round.

Application deadlines

The 2027 scholarship application closes 25 November 2026 at 12:00 PST, after the published extension. Degree admission has its own deadline.

How to apply

1. Check the award and degree entry rules. 2. Start the award form and invite the institutional nominator and teacher referee. 3. Complete the financial and applicant sections and submit by the award cutoff. 4. Submit the UBC degree application separately and follow the document deadlines.

Required documents

Prepare academic and financial evidence. Request an institutional nomination and a teacher reference through the award portal.
