Schwarzman Scholars at Tsinghua University

Destination: China · Study level: Master

Funding coverage

Fully funded one-year master’s programme at Tsinghua University. Read the offer to confirm personal and family expenses outside the award.

Eligibility

Bangladeshi applicants generally use the global route. Meet the cohort-specific age, undergraduate degree and English requirements. The 2028/29 eligibility checker asks whether applicants were born after 1 August 1999 and will complete their degree by 1 August 2028.

Application deadlines

The 2027/28 round is closed. The provider lists April–September 2027 for global applications to the 2028/29 cohort. Exact dates are pending.

How to apply

1. Check eligibility for the global round. 2. Gather academic records and references. 3. Complete the application in English and submit electronically. 4. Follow the portal’s authorship rules and attend selection if shortlisted.
