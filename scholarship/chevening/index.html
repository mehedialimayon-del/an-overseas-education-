Chevening Scholarship · UK One-Year Master

Destination: United Kingdom · Study level: Master

Funding coverage

Tuition, approved economy return flights, visa fee, arrival/departure support and monthly living stipend; stipend varies between London and non-London and by award year.

Eligibility

Bangladeshi citizens with a first degree, demonstrated leadership and required post-degree work experience, eligible one-year UK master’s choices and required home-return commitment.

Programme scope

Illustrative UK university choices; most eligible one-year taught master’s courses across UK providers, subject to Chevening terms.

Duration

One year.

Application deadline

2027/28 Bangladesh Chevening applications are closed (October 2026); next cycle is not open yet.

Required documents

Three eligible UK course choices, education records, employment history, leadership/career essays and referee details; offer and language rules of host course apply.
