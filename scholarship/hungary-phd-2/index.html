Stipendium Hungaricum · Doctorate Phase II

Hungary · PhD

Funding coverage

HUF 180,000/month doctoral phase II (normally semesters 5–8); tuition waived, separate housing contribution or dormitory and medical support.

Eligibility

Continuing eligible doctoral scholarship holders after first-phase progress; not a separate first-entry doctoral scholarship.

Programme scope

Use the same official doctorate host and project; this entry isolates the second funding rate.

Duration

Normally doctoral semesters 5–8.

Application deadline

Confirm the current application deadline on the official scholarship website.

How to apply

Apply first through a doctoral Stipendium Hungaricum call. Phase II stipend follows qualifying progression and scholarship rules, not a second independent application.
