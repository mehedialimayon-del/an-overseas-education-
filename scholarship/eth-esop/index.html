ETH Zurich Excellence & Opportunity Scholarship

Destination: Switzerland · Study level: Master

Funding coverage

CHF 13,500 per semester from autumn 2027 for study and living expenses, plus a tuition waiver for the regular master’s duration of three or four semesters.

Eligibility

Strong bachelor’s results, around the top 10% of the cohort. Apply for an eligible ETH master’s degree. Master of Advanced Studies programmes are outside this scheme. Existing ETH master’s students and applicants who already hold a master’s degree are ineligible.

Application deadlines

Autumn 2027 entry: 1–30 November 2026, closing at 11:59 CET on 30 November.

How to apply

1. Select the eligible master’s programme. 2. Open eApply during the international window and select ESOP in the master’s application. 3. Add the thesis pre-proposal and referee details where required. 4. Submit by the published deadline and remain reachable for possible interview.

Required documents

ETH master’s application, original thesis pre-proposal. If the degree does not request references, nominate two professors for ESOP and have them submit letters within two weeks.
