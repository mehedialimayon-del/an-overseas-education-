Malaysia International Scholarship (MIS)

Malaysia · Master / PhD

Funding coverage

Approved tuition plus RM 1,500/month living allowance; no government-funded international airfare.

Eligibility

Bangladesh included under Commonwealth recipient-country route; eligible master/PhD applicants must satisfy degree, English, age and university criteria of MIS 2026.

Programme scope

Illustrative MIS-listed institutions; use its official PDF for complete 2026 approved public and private university list.

Application deadline

MIS 2026/27 applications closed; award checking 8 Oct–31 Dec 2026.

Fees and conditions

MIS scholarship application fee not separately stated; any Malaysian university admission and EMGS/visa fees are separate.
