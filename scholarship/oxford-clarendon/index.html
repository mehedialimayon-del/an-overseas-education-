Oxford Clarendon Scholarship

Destination: United Kingdom · Study level: Master / PhD

Funding coverage

Full course fees and a living-cost grant for eligible full-time scholars. Part-time awards offer a study-support grant. The funding period follows the fee-liability period.

Eligibility

New Oxford master’s and DPhil applicants from any nationality, including Bangladesh. Selection is competitive and based on academic merit and potential.

Application deadlines

Apply by the relevant December or January funding deadline for your Oxford course. Each course has its own date.

How to apply

1. Find the Oxford graduate course and its funding deadline. 2. Complete the course application and required documents before that deadline. 3. Clarendon consideration is automatic. 4. Check any separate university, college or external awards individually.
