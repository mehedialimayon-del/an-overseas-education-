NCKU Distinguished International Graduate Scholarship

Taiwan · Master / PhD

Funding coverage

Tuition reduction and monthly award up to NT$10,000 for Master or NT$20,000 for PhD; competitive institutional assessment.

Eligibility

Open to international applicants including Bangladesh who meet NCKU graduate admission, academic and review criteria.

Programme scope

National Cheng Kung University only.

Application deadline

Confirm the current application deadline on the official scholarship website.

How to apply

Apply for NCKU graduate admission; university evaluates first-year scholarship together with the admission without a separate scholarship application.
