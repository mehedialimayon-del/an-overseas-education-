NUS Research Scholarship

Destination: Singapore · Study level: Master by Research / PhD

Funding coverage

Tuition subsidy and a monthly stipend. The international rate effective January 2026 is SGD 2,900 for a research master’s and SGD 3,000 for a PhD. Qualifying PhD candidates may receive a performance-dependent supplement after the qualifying exam.

Eligibility

International applicants can be considered for eligible NUS graduate research programmes. Check the department’s degree, language and research entry requirements.

Application deadlines

Apply according to the graduate programme and intake. NUS does not publish a single deadline for every department.

How to apply

1. Select the NUS research programme and intake. 2. Check the department’s eligibility, research fit and funding instructions. 3. Indicate your interest in the scholarship in the graduate application where requested. 4. Review the written award, renewal and assistantship obligations.
