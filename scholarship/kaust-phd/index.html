Destination: Saudi Arabia · Level: PhD

Funding coverage

KAUST PhD admitted students receive tuition, housing and medical/dental support plus living allowance; published allowance range USD 20,000–30,000/year based on stage. USD 30,000 shown is the upper bound, not guaranteed to every entrant.

Eligibility

Bangladeshi researchers meeting KAUST graduate research admission, supervisor match and academic criteria.
