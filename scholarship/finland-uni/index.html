Finnish University Tuition-Fee Scholarships

Finland · Bachelor / Master

Funding coverage

Competitive university-specific tuition waiver, partially or fully; ordinarily no living-cost funding.

Eligibility

Bangladeshi non-EU applicants to an eligible Finnish university degree, meeting academic/language requirements and each scholarship’s criteria.

Programme scope

Apply through each Finnish university. Finland has no general government Bachelor/Master scholarship programme.

Application deadline

Check the latest official provider announcement; no verified current deadline is recorded.
