ADB–Japan Scholarship Program (ADB-JSP)

Destination: Asia-Pacific (multiple countries) · Study level: Master

Funding coverage

Full approved tuition, living allowance, books, medical insurance, travel and eligible research costs; amounts vary by institution.

Eligibility

Bangladesh is an ADB borrowing country route; superior bachelor’s results, usually 2 years full-time work, English proficiency and age normally ≤35; must return home for ≥2 years.

Programme scope

Only 27 approved ADB-JSP institutions and listed approved master’s programmes count (2026 ADB material). The official ADB institution list is definitive.

Application deadline

The current application deadline must be confirmed on the official provider website; no verified date is listed in the current catalogue record.
