HKU Presidential PhD Scholar Programme

Destination: Hong Kong · Level: PhD

Funding coverage

2027/28 selected scholars: HK$28,700/month postgraduate scholarship, plus HK$40,000 first-year cash award, HK$20,000 later years, waived composition fees and conference travel support. Official package up to HK$445,800 first year.

Eligibility

Outstanding full-time PhD applicants from any country including Bangladesh. Competitive admission, research proposal and interviews may apply.

Application timeline and documents

For 2027/28 HKU encourages main round application between 1 September and 1 December 2026.

Research proposal, transcripts, degree qualifications, two academic referees, English test where required, CV/publications.
