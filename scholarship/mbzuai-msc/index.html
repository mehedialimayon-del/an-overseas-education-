MBZUAI Funded MSc · Artificial Intelligence

Destination: United Arab Emirates · Level: Master

Funding coverage

Eligible fully funded MBZUAI MSc students: AED 15,500/month international stipend, tuition waiver, eligible residence visa, health cover and one annual return flight. The Master in Applied Artificial Intelligence (MAAI) is fee-paying and excluded.

Eligibility

International STEM graduates including Bangladesh who meet MBZUAI MSc entry and academic programme criteria; 2027 scholarship terms apply to qualifying full-time MSc.

Application timeline and documents

MBZUAI 2027 site lists 15 December 2026 for most graduate programmes, with certain programmes later.

STEM degree and transcripts, English tests per programme, statement of purpose, referees, CV, and application/interview materials.
