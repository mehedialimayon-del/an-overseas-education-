Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme · HKU Track

Hong Kong · PhD

Funding coverage

Competitive Research Grants Council Hong Kong PhD Fellowship; HKU says 2027/28 HKPFS holders also receive its Presidential PhD package and composition fee waiver. Exact combined monthly allowance and bonuses are in current RGC/HKU award schedule.

Eligibility

Research-excellent international doctoral applicants including Bangladeshi citizens with admission to participating Hong Kong PhD institution; HKU track shown here.

Programme scope

This entry covers only HKU-hosted HKPFS; other Hong Kong universities host their own awards.

Application deadline

2027/28 initial RGC system: before 1 December 2026 at 12:00 Hong Kong time; HKU submission follows.

Application steps

1. Register at HKPFS electronic system and obtain reference before deadline. 2. Apply to HKU PhD using that reference. 3. Submit research proposal, recommendations and course-specific documents. 4. Attend selection if invited.
