Stanford Knight-Hennessy Scholars

Destination: United States · Study level: Master / PhD

Funding coverage

Up to three years of support for the approved Stanford graduate degree, including tuition and associated fees, a living and academic stipend, and an annual travel stipend. Funding in later years depends on the degree programme.

Eligibility

Open to applicants from all countries, including Bangladesh, who meet the first-degree timing rule for their cohort and gain admission to an eligible full-time Stanford graduate programme.

Application deadlines

The 2027 scholarship round closed on 6 October 2026. Stanford announces that the 2028 scholarship application will open in summer 2027. Stanford degree applications have separate dates.

How to apply

1. Check the degree timing and graduate programme requirements. 2. Prepare a separate Knight-Hennessy application. 3. Submit the Stanford graduate degree application by the applicable deadline for scholarship candidates. 4. Confirm both admission decisions and written funding beyond year three if relevant.
