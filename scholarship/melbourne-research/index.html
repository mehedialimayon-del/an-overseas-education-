Melbourne Graduate Research Scholarships

Destination: Australia · Study level: Master by Research / PhD

Funding coverage

The stipend and fee-offset route includes fee remission, a living allowance at the published 2027 rate, AUD 3,000 relocation support for students moving from overseas and single OSHC for eligible international recipients. Fee-offset-only awards also exist. Rates may change annually.

Eligibility

International applicants may qualify for eligible graduate research degrees. Selection considers prior academic results and research potential.

Application deadlines

New applicants who submit by the graduate research course deadline are considered automatically. Current or deferred students may have a separate application process.

How to apply

1. Select the research degree and check faculty entry rules. 2. Confirm any supervisor or project requirements. 3. Submit the research degree application by the faculty deadline. 4. Check the award letter for whether it includes stipend, fees or both.
