Stipendium Hungaricum · Doctorate Phase I

Hungary · PhD

Funding coverage

Tuition-free doctoral training; HUF 140,000/month for first four semesters, plus accommodation contribution HUF 40,000/month or eligible dorm and insurance.

Eligibility

Relevant postgraduate qualification; eligible Bangladesh sending partner route, doctoral host/supervisor and research proposal.

Programme scope

Only doctoral institutions and research topics published in the application portal.

Duration

First four semesters of ordinarily 8-semester doctorate.

Application deadline

Confirm the current application deadline on the official scholarship website.

Required documents

Bachelor/Master records, research plan, supervisor acceptance where needed, language results, recommendations and passport.
