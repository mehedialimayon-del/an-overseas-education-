Chinese Government Scholarship · Type A Bachelor

China · Bachelor

Funding coverage

Approx. CNY 2,500/month, full tuition, on-campus accommodation, insurance and — as announced for Bangladesh Type A 2026 — round-trip flights.

Eligibility

Bangladeshi nationals explicitly invited in the Chinese Embassy’s Type A call; current academic, age, language and embassy procedure must be checked.

Programme scope

Eligible Chinese university programme choices are controlled by the CSC system and annual Chinese Embassy notices.

Application deadline

The Bangladesh 2026 Type A call closed 10 January 2026; check the next embassy notice.
