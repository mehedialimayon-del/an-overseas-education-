GREAT Scholarships · Bangladesh UK

United Kingdom · Master

Funding coverage

At least £10,000 tuition contribution to an eligible one-year taught UK postgraduate course; living expenses and remaining tuition may be self-funded.

Eligibility

Explicitly for Bangladeshi citizens with a first degree who meet the participating university’s 2026/27 entry and award rules.

Programme scope

British Council published 7 GREAT Bangladesh scholarships in 2026/27; open its page for the entire university list (not just the examples here).

Application deadline

2026/27 opportunities; each host has its own deadline.

Fees and conditions

University programme application fees may apply; the tuition discount is not cash paid to the student.
