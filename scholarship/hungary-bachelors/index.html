Stipendium Hungaricum · Bachelor / One-Tier

Hungary · Bachelor

Funding coverage

Tuition exemption + HUF 43,700/month + free available dormitory OR HUF 40,000/month housing contribution + health support; not full living costs.

Eligibility

Available to eligible Bangladeshi nationals subject to the Bangladesh sending partner’s annual nomination and field quota; programme requirements still apply.

Programme scope

The current Stipendium Hungaricum application portal displays eligible Hungarian host institutions, programmes and Bangladesh-specific fields.

Duration

Usually 3–6 years depending on course.

Application deadline

Confirm the current application deadline on the official scholarship website.

Required documents

Passport, secondary-school academic results, motivation, health certificate when requested, English/local language evidence and sending-partner documentation.
