Government of Brunei Darussalam · Foreign Students Scholarship

Brunei · Diploma / Bachelor / Master

Funding coverage

The 2026/27 award included tuition and institutional charges, BND 500 monthly personal allowance, BND 150 monthly meal allowance, BND 600 yearly book allowance, campus accommodation, eligible flights and insurance. These are historical terms, not a confirmed offer for the next intake.

Programme scope

The Brunei MFA annual approved university and programme list is definitive.

Application deadline

Check the latest official provider announcement; no verified current deadline is recorded.

Eligibility

For the 2026/27 call, first-time international tertiary students could apply. Age on 1 July of the course-start year: up to 25 for Diploma or Bachelor, up to 35 for a Master. The call excludes Brunei citizens, permanent residents and international students who previously attended a Brunei tertiary institution.

Language requirements

For the 2026/27 call, IELTS 6.0, TOEFL 550, GCE O-Level English Credit 6 or IGCSE English as a second language grade C were listed. A general medium-of-instruction waiver was not stated.

Application documents

Identity card, certified birth certificate and passport, certified certificates and transcripts, certified English translations where required, and referee forms C1 and C2.

Application steps

Choose an eligible programme at a participating institution; prepare certified documents and references; submit the MFA scholarship application; complete separate UBD or UTB university applications where required; attend any requested assessment.

Deadline and status

The 2026/27 call closed on 15 February 2026. The next deadline has not been confirmed in the catalogue. Do not treat this award as currently open.

Programme conditions

The 2026/27 terms did not provide spouse or child allowances, prohibited paid employment during the award, and required recipients to leave Brunei after programme completion. Verify the new call before relying on these conditions.
