Qatar University International Students Scholarship

Destination: Qatar · Level: Bachelor

Funding coverage

Full tuition and textbooks waiver, QAR 500 monthly, shared university housing with transport, annual return air ticket and QU-sponsored residence permit.

Eligibility

Non-Qatari international candidates including Bangladesh, first undergraduate degree only, normally ≥95% secondary result and final admission. Transfers/second bachelor degree not eligible.

Application timeline and documents

For 2027/28 HKU encourages main round application between 1 September and 1 December 2026.

Application documents required for admission including secondary transcripts, identity and any test score required by the intended college.
