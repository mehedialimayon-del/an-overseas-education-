Advertised €2,100/month since January 2026 on main Campus France page; qualified additional transport and insurance provisions. Check 2027 award letter (implementation pages have differed). Tuition is not generally funded by Eiffel itself.

Eligibility

Foreign national including Bangladesh, normally age ≤35 and an eligible French host/joint doctoral arrangement in priority fields.

Programme scope

Only French institution nomination; direct student submission to Campus France invalid.

Duration

According to 2027 institutional PhD terms.

Application deadline

Institutional national submission deadline 6 January 2027; internal host deadlines earlier.

Required documents

Research plan, supervisor/doctoral school support, degree evidence, research CV, referees and institution nominee forms.
