University of Toronto Lester B. Pearson Scholarship

Destination: Canada · Study level: Bachelor

Funding coverage

Four years of tuition, books, incidental fees and residence support for selected international undergraduates.

Eligibility

International first-degree candidates must meet the school-leaving rules for their cohort. A secondary school can nominate one student per year. Students already attending post-secondary studies are excluded.

Application deadlines

For September 2027 entry, school nomination closed 9 October 2026, OUAC admission 16 October, and the award form 6 November. The next cohort’s dates are pending.

How to apply

1. Ask your secondary school about nomination. 2. Submit an eligible U of T undergraduate application through OUAC. 3. Use the scholarship application instructions provided to nominated applicants. 4. Submit the award form and supporting material by the separate deadline.
