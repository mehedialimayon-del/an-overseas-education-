HKU Postgraduate Scholarship · Research Degrees

Destination: Hong Kong · Level: MPhil / PhD

Funding coverage

HKU PGS basic HK$19,135/month from September 2026 for eligible research degree holders; confirmed PhD probation may increase to HK$19,655/month. Annual renewal depends on progress and funds.

Eligibility

Bangladeshi/international MPhil or PhD students meeting HKU full-time research admission and scholarship merit requirements; normally strong first degree.

Application timeline and documents

2027/28 initial RGC system: before 1 December 2026 at 12:00 Hong Kong time; HKU submission follows.

Research field and supervisor fit, academic certificates, transcripts, references, English evidence and university admission forms.
